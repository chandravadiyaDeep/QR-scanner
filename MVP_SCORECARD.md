# MVP Prioritisation Scorecard

Score candidates independently for restaurants and cinemas. Use evidence, not intuition alone.

## Dimensions (1–5)
Economic impact; frequency; user pain; evidence confidence; strategic fit; feasibility (5 = easier); risk (5 = lower risk); dependency load (5 = fewer external dependencies).

A safety, financial, privacy or concurrency blocker cannot be averaged away.

## Restaurant candidate workflows
QR menu/live availability; table identification/self-order; idempotent order acceptance; kitchen routing/status; waitlist/seating; staff assistance/exception handling; payment/refunds; operational reporting.

## Cinema candidate workflows
Film/showtime discovery; live seat map/atomic holds; booking/payment; secure ticket issuance; admission scan/replay prevention; showtime change/cancellation; concession pre-order; queue/occupancy/conversion reporting.

## MVP entry gate
A workflow enters MVP only when the problem is validated; a buyer confirms material value and pilot willingness; workflow and exception paths are mapped; integrations/dependencies are known; risks have mitigations; acceptance criteria and baseline metrics exist; and a complete end-to-end slice is feasible.

For each decision record vertical, evidence, score, dependencies, owner, rationale, acceptance criteria and revisit trigger. Product owner approves final scope; scores do not auto-approve features.
