# Customer Discovery Playbook

Run independent restaurant and cinema research tracks. Do not flatten them into a generic hospitality persona.

## Objectives
Understand real workflows; identify repeated manual work and its cost; quantify queueing, abandonment and rework; map existing systems and constraints; identify buyer, user, approver and support owner.

## Participants
For each vertical: owner/buyer, operations manager, frontline staff and guests. Include current POS/ticketing/IT provider when relevant.

## Restaurant questions
1. Walk through busy service from arrival to exit.
2. How are tables and waitlists managed?
3. How many staff minutes are spent taking, entering and correcting orders?
4. Where are orders delayed, lost or miscommunicated?
5. How are unavailable items communicated?
6. What are normal and peak wait times?
7. Which POS, printer and payment systems are used?
8. Which exceptions need a manager?
9. What do errors, remakes, voids and abandoned guests cost?
10. What would stop staff or guests using QR/self-order?

## Cinema questions
1. Walk through ticket purchase, seat allocation and auditorium entry.
2. What causes peak queues and how are they staffed?
3. How are holds, cancellations, refunds and showtime changes handled?
4. How are tickets validated and replay prevented?
5. How do concession queues affect experience and revenue?
6. Which systems control showtimes, seat inventory, ticketing and POS?
7. What happens during internet, scanner or payment outages?
8. Which reports do managers need?
9. What fraud, accessibility and support cases occur?
10. What integration or contract restrictions exist?

## Observation
Record venue type, date/time, service period, approximate volume, staffing by role, queue length over time, task steps, handoffs, errors, workarounds and exception paths. Obtain permission; avoid unnecessary personal data collection.

## Quantification
Estimate staff minutes per transaction × transactions per period; loaded labour cost per minute; queue time and abandonment; error/rework rate and cost; revenue contribution/loss where measurable; current software/provider/hardware cost. Label assumptions clearly.

Tag findings as Observed, Reported, Estimated or Hypothesis. Seek disconfirming evidence. Do not generalise anecdotes into market-wide claims.

## Outputs
Personas and stakeholder map; current-state workflow and exception map; problem statements with frequency/severity/cost; baseline KPI sheet; system/integration map; candidate future-state workflow; pilot criteria and risks; decision log.
