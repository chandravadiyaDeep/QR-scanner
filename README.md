# Restaurant + Cinema Operations Platform

A business operations platform focused on exactly two industries: restaurants and cinemas.

Our mission is to reduce avoidable operating costs and guest friction: queues, manual order-taking, repetitive staff work, order/booking errors, slow service and disconnected operations.

## Start here in every chat
1. Read CURRENT_STATE.md first.
2. Read AGENTS.md for operating rules.
3. Read COMMANDS.md for reusable commands.
4. Read PRODUCT_PLAN.md for product scope.

## Two focused products, one platform
- Restaurant OS: digital menu, QR guest ordering, waitlist, order routing, kitchen workflow, service coordination and insights.
- Cinema OS: showtimes, seat selection/booking, secure digital tickets, admission validation, concession pre-orders and insights.

Shared identity, tenancy, roles, notifications, audit, media and reporting do not erase domain-specific workflows.
